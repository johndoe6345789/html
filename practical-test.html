Force feedback

Browsers hand steering and pedal input to web pages, but not wheel force feedback. The page computes the forces every frame (self-centring that builds with speed, kerb strikes, verge rumble, collisions) and sends them to whichever outputs work on your setup.

Uses WebHID; works on wheels that speak standard HID force feedback.
